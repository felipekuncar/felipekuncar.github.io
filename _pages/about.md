I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [University of Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.ac.nz), in Christchurch, New Zealand. My research sits at the intersection of geotechnical earthquake engineering and engineering seismology. By combining observations from past earthquakes, geotechnical data, and numerical simulations, I aim to improve our ability to predict earthquake-induced ground motion and, ultimately, enhance the resilience of the built environment to future events.

I hold a PhD in Earthquake Engineering from the [University of Canterbury](https://www.canterbury.ac.nz) and earned both my Master of Science and Professional Civil Engineering degrees from [Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María](https://usm.cl/en/home/), in Valparaíso, Chile. I have conducted research stays at [Virginia Tech](https://www.vt.edu/) and the [University of Arizona](https://www.arizona.edu/) in the United States. Before beginning my PhD, I spent several years as a Project Engineer at [Ferrara](https://ferrara.cl/) in Santiago, Chile, where I participated in a wide range of geotechnical engineering projects across the country. My work encompassed leading site investigations, developing geotechnical reports, assessing soil liquefaction potential, and designing deep foundations, soil improvement techniques, and support systems for deep excavations.
